# Product: Cortex

Cortex is a privacy-first, ASD-friendly AI copilot for engineering work. It runs locally on NVIDIA GPU hardware and provides task planning, meeting analysis, summarization, and stakeholder communication with predictable, low-cognitive-load outputs.

## Design Principles

- Predictable structure: responses follow consistent formats
- Explicit over implicit: unknowns and ambiguity are always surfaced
- Low cognitive load: bullet points, clear sections, no filler
- Deterministic outputs: low temperature LLM for consistent responses
- No context switching: one system for planning, meetings, and knowledge
